This file is what makes EverythingImages portable: with it here, EverythingImages keeps its
index, its settings and the AI models it downloads in the "data" folder beside
EverythingImages.exe, instead of in your Windows user folders. The whole folder is the
app: copy it to another PC or a USB drive and everything comes too, delete it
and nothing is left behind.

Delete this file to use the normal per-user folders instead
(%APPDATA%\EverythingImages).

EverythingImages needs the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime. If this PC does not have it,
EverythingImages.exe says so when started and offers the download, or get it here:
https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0
