## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-18

The first public release.

### Added
- Instant search across your image folders by filename, text in the images
  (Windows OCR), dominant colours and AI descriptions.
- AI descriptions from Liquid AI's small vision models, run on this PC's GPU
  (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, through the driver already installed) or on the CPU.
  Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- Filters (has text, landscape, portrait, file type), grid and list views, and
  an inspector showing each image's colours, text and description.
- Portable: the app keeps its index, settings and models in a `data` folder
  beside itself, so the whole folder can move to another PC or a USB drive.
- About window (ⓘ) with the version and links.
